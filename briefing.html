OpenAI launches GPT-5.5 on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 — Codex now powered by latest frontier model

GPT-5.5 went live May 5 with more than 10,000 NVIDIANs across functions getting early access. The model runs on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack-scale systems, and Codex — OpenAI's agentic coding application — is now powered by GPT-5.5 across the CLI, desktop app and Visual Studio Code extension. The launch reaffirms NVL72 as the unit of frontier-AI compute.

AWS and OpenAI expand Bedrock partnership — GPT-5.5 and 5.4 land on Bedrock APIs, Codex in limited preview

AWS confirmed an expanded partnership bringing the latest OpenAI frontier models — GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4 — onto Amazon Bedrock APIs. Codex on Bedrock is in limited preview, with the OpenAI coding agent reachable via the Bedrock API plus the Codex CLI, desktop app and VS Code extension.

TSMC · CAPEX
2nmA13 / N2UCoWoS-L

TSMC ramps capex to chase AI demand surge — 2nm in production, A13 / N2U landing for 2027 accelerators

TSMC continues to step up spending plans as AI capex absorbs leading-edge capacity. The foundry started 2nm production late last year, leading Samsung and Intel; CoWoS-L advanced packaging remains the binding constraint for NVIDIA, AMD and hyperscaler accelerators. Rivals are narrowing the technology gap, but TSMC still controls the supply curve into the 2027 accelerator cycle.

18A yieldPanther Lake14A unproven

Intel manufacturing capacity issues "may take years to fix" — foundry credibility back under the microscope

Reporting flagged that Intel's manufacturing capacity gap with TSMC could take years to close, with 18A yields under scrutiny and 14A still unproven for external customers. The Panther Lake (Core Ultra) launch remains the make-or-break read on whether Intel Foundry can win the next round of marquee clients while AMD and NVIDIA keep accelerating their TSMC-anchored roadmaps.

AI & Policy 03
GOOGLE · ANTHROPIC
Alphabet$40B5GW compute

Alphabet's $40B Anthropic investment — first $10B at $350B valuation, 5GW of compute dedicated

Alphabet announced a multi-tranche investment of up to $40 billion in Anthropic, kicking off with a $10 billion check at a $350 billion valuation, with subsequent tranches gated by milestones. Alphabet is dedicating 5 gigawatts of computing capacity to the company. Anthropic's annualized revenue has scaled from roughly $1B at end-2024 to $9B at end-2025 to about $30B by early April 2026, with an IPO reportedly under consideration later this year.

EXPORT · CONTROLS
H200ChinaBIS

No H200s in China yet — White House warns on "industrial-scale" model distillation

Despite the January 2026 BIS rule moving to case-by-case license review for H200- and MI325X-equivalent chips, no H200s have actually shipped to China as of May 2026. In late April the White House publicly warned that Beijing is running "deliberate, industrial-scale campaigns" to distill U.S. frontier AI systems — complicating any near-term licensing wave and reviving experts' calls to halt advanced AI chip exports outright.

WHITE HOUSE · COUNCIL
HuangLisa SuAltman / Musk excluded

Trump tech advisory council seats Huang, Su, Zuckerberg and Ellison — Altman and Musk left off the list

The chip-and-platform-led group is steering U.S. AI policy. Notable absences: OpenAI's Sam Altman, any Microsoft executives and Elon Musk — leaving the agentic-AI champion outside the formal advisory perimeter. Lisa Su's seat reinforces AMD's policy footprint as MI400 ramps; Huang remains the council's central technical voice.
