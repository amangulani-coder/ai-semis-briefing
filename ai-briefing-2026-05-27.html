Lead Stories 03
FUNDING · AI CODING
Cognition$1B$26B valuation

Cognition raises $1B at $26B valuation as Devin maker becomes the agentic-coding bellwether

Lux Capital, General Catalyst and 8VC co-led the round, with Ribbit, Atreides and Peter Thiel's Founders Fund joining. The valuation has more than doubled since September and crystallizes the bid for agentic coding stacks — Cognition's Devin sits alongside Cursor and Codex as the platforms enterprises now budget around. The deal also flags how aggressively growth investors are still chasing AI infrastructure plays even at lofty multiples.

POLICY · WORKFORCE
OpenAI Foundation$250MJobs

OpenAI Foundation commits $250M to help workers adapt to AI disruption

The newly capitalized foundation will fund retraining, education and direct grants targeted at workers most exposed to AI-driven job displacement. It is OpenAI's most concrete answer yet to the labor-impact critique that has dogged the company, and it lands the same week peers including Anthropic and Meta are raising or spending at unprecedented scale. Expect policy makers to use the program as a benchmark.

FUNDING · FRONTIER LABS
Anthropic$30B raise$900B+ val

Anthropic closes second $30B round of 2026, valuation tops $900B

Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter and Greenoaks led the Growth round, the second $30B check Anthropic has banked this year. ARR has moved from $14B in February to roughly $30B in April, putting the lab on a revenue trajectory that rivals OpenAI's. The capital is earmarked for Claude training capacity and Mythos, the strategic-reasoning model unveiled earlier in May.

Jensen Huang commits $150B annually to Taiwan, calls the island the AI epicenter

At Computex week, Huang told a Taipei audience NVIDIA will pour up to $150B a year into Taiwan operations and break ground on a new Taipei headquarters in 2027. Shares dipped premarket on geopolitical-concentration concerns before recovering, settling near $206 with GB200 NVL72 still backlogged through 2027. Vera Rubin remains the marquee 2H26 roadmap catalyst.

AMD · SAMSUNG · META
AMDSamsung 2nm6GW Instinct

Lisa Su pledges $10B to Taiwan, nears Samsung 2nm deal as Meta locks in 6GW of Instinct GPUs

Su's Taipei visit produced two headline commitments: a $10B Taiwan investment to expand manufacturing partnerships, and an accelerated push toward Samsung Foundry's 2nm node to diversify beyond TSMC. The kicker is a Meta deal covering up to 6GW of AMD Instinct deployment, which Su says puts AMD on track for tens of billions in data-center AI revenue by 2027.

INTEL · SAMSUNG
INTC +6%Apple foundrySilicon photonics

Intel pops on Apple foundry chatter as Samsung opens silicon-photonics foundry

Intel shares jumped on reports that Apple is in advanced talks to use Intel Foundry for a slice of its silicon, a long-rumored move that would validate 18A. Separately, Samsung launched pilot production of silicon-photonics modules for AI optical interconnect, with mass production targeted for 2H26 — a direct play against TSMC's CoWoS bottleneck.

AI & Policy 03
META · CAPEX
Muse Spark$115-135BSuperintelligence Labs

Meta unveils Muse Spark and locks in $115-135B AI capex for 2026

Muse Spark is the first flagship LLM out of Alexandr Wang's new Superintelligence Labs, with multimodal and agentic benchmarks competitive with frontier peers at a fraction of Llama 4's compute cost. The model debut shipped alongside guidance that 2026 AI infrastructure spend will land in the $115-135B range — nearly double last year — putting Meta in the same capex bracket as Google and Microsoft.

Google DeepMind acquires Contextual AI team via $80-90M licensing wrapper

DeepMind brought over the entire Contextual AI engineering bench through a licensing structure explicitly designed to dodge merger-review thresholds — a playbook now familiar from the Inflection, Adept and Character.AI deals. Regulators on both sides of the Atlantic have flagged these acqui-license arrangements; expect this transaction to draw fresh scrutiny.

Anthropic ships Mythos reasoning model as Jack Clark pegs 60% odds of self-improving AI by 2028

Mythos is positioned for complex strategic planning and multi-step reasoning workloads where Claude has historically traded on safety-tuned outputs. In the same week, head of policy Jack Clark publicly put a 60% probability on recursively self-improving systems by 2028 — a forecast that lands as Washington weighs new compute-disclosure rules.
