Pentagon clears 8 AI vendors for classified networks — Anthropic shut out over autonomous-weapons clause

DoD finalized agreements with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle, NVIDIA, SpaceX and Reflection AI to deploy models inside Impact Level 6 and 7 environments. Tiger Global and GV led the round; Sierra builds LLM-powered customer-service agents now deployed across Fortune 50 enterprises. The check nearly doubles total funding and arrives as agentic platforms continue to absorb a disproportionate share of late-stage capital.

Intel 18A yields under scrutiny — Panther Lake debut becomes the credibility test for Intel Foundry

DoD opens classified networks to AI: Reflection AI joins the eight, hyperscalers cement classified-cloud footing

The IL6/IL7 agreements cover mission planning, intelligence analysis, logistics and weapons-targeting workflows. Reflection AI — backed by NVIDIA and founded by ex-Google DeepMind researchers — is the surprise inclusion alongside OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle, NVIDIA and SpaceX.

Trump tech advisory council seats Huang, Su, Zuckerberg and Ellison — Altman, Musk excluded

The roster sets up a chip-and-platform-led group steering U.S. AI policy. Notable absences: OpenAI's Sam Altman, any Microsoft executives, and Elon Musk — leaving the agentic-AI champion outside the formal advisory perimeter.

Anthropic v. DoD: court block stands, but commercial trail-offs continue as White House reopens dialogue

Reporting indicates the White House quietly resumed conversations after Anthropic's recent technology announcements.
